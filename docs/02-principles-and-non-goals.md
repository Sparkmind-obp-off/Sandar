# 02 — Principles and Non-goals

## Principles
1. **Agency over automation.** The user sets the goal and chooses whether to act.
2. **Data minimization.** Ask only for information needed for a user-requested feature.
3. **Purpose limitation.** A value entered for one task is not silently reused for another.
4. **Explainability.** Show why a suggestion appeared, its assumptions, source, freshness, and uncertainty.
5. **Universal dignity.** No poverty score, failure label, moral judgment, or ranking of human worth.
6. **Inclusive defaults.** Support irregular income, cash and non-cash resources, different household arrangements, accessibility needs, and low-bandwidth use.
7. **Reversibility.** Users can edit, pause, export, or delete their own workspace data.
8. **Human control.** External actions require a review screen and explicit confirmation.
9. **Source integrity.** Public information is attributed to its issuer and never presented as an official decision.
10. **Cost discipline.** Rp0 is the operating default; paid dependencies require explicit approval first.

## Non-goals
- Not a native computer operating system.
- Not a social-media monitoring, identity-resolution, people-search, or surveillance tool.
- Not a credit score, risk score, insurance underwriting, or loan eligibility engine.
- Not an automated government-benefit eligibility decision.
- Not a medical, mental-health, legal, tax, or investment adviser.
- Not a debt collector, lender, broker, employer, recruiter, or government representative.
- Not a family-control application; no tracking of another adult without their informed participation.
- Not a lead-generation product that sells user information.
- Not an AI system that infers sensitive traits from names, photos, accounts, or behavior.

## Hard prohibitions
Never investigate, profile, contact, or infer the circumstances of a named person who has not opted in. Never use private-account access, credential collection, covert tracking, or scraped personal profiles to populate a user's life context. Never submit an application, message, payment, booking, or data disclosure without exact user approval.
