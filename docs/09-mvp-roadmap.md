# 09 — MVP Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation (current)
- Product vision, principles, non-goals, module boundaries.
- Privacy and safety baseline.
- Conceptual data model and architecture direction.
- Research and source policy.
- Release gates and unresolved questions.

Exit: documentation reviewed and owner-approved.

## Phase 1 — Private planning workspace
- Responsive shell and accessible navigation.
- Workspace creation without public profile.
- User-entered goals and basic cash-flow items.
- Deterministic cash-flow arithmetic and simple scenarios.
- Export and delete controls.

Exit: synthetic-data tests pass; authorization and deletion tests pass; no external integrations required.

## Phase 2 — Curated public resource navigator
- Admin-curated public resource catalogue.
- Provenance and freshness fields.
- Search by topic and user-selected jurisdiction.
- Official-source handoff and stale-entry reporting.

Exit: each published record has a primary source, coverage, verification date, and caveats.

## Phase 3 — Opportunity and skills navigator
- User-entered skills/interests.
- Public opportunity catalogue with cost, deadline, and prerequisites where verified.
- Comparison and preparation checklist.

Exit: no automated applications; no private profile scraping.

## Phase 4 — Optional shared planning
- Shared spaces, invitations, acceptance, field-level permissions.
- Leave/revoke controls and audit trail.

Exit: tests prove that unaccepted invitees cannot access data and private records do not leak.

## Phase 5 — Capacity and future resilience
- Optional check-ins and workload planning.
- User-owned contingency plans and review reminders.

Exit: no diagnostic language or automated risk classification.

## Phase 6 — Controlled pilot
- Small opt-in cohort with clear notice and support route.
- Synthetic test data before real data.
- Incident response, backup restore, and privacy review.
- Measure usefulness and comprehension, not personal vulnerability.

Exit: pilot review and explicit release approval.

## Deferred
- Bank integrations, payment initiation, automated applications, messaging, health integrations, predictive scoring, public social features, and paid AI/search APIs.
