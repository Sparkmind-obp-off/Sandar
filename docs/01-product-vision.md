# 01 — Product Vision

## One-sentence definition
SANDAR is a privacy-first Life Navigation & Resilience OS that helps a person turn a changing real-life situation into clear, manageable next steps by organizing their own information, finding trustworthy resources, exploring opportunities, and preparing for uncertainty.

## The problem space
People often have to coordinate several connected decisions at once: money coming in and going out, bills, household responsibilities, available public services, learning or work opportunities, energy and time, and future contingencies. These needs are fragmented across notes, spreadsheets, websites, agencies, and conversations.

SANDAR aims to reduce that fragmentation without pretending that one application can solve structural problems or replace human support.

## Desired outcome
A user should be able to answer:
- What is happening in the area of life I chose to work on?
- What matters first, and why?
- Which resources or options may be relevant?
- What is the smallest useful next step?
- What information is missing or uncertain?
- What should I review later?

## Product posture
- A decision-support and navigation layer, not an authority that decides for the user.
- A private workspace by default, not a public social network.
- A modular toolkit, not a mandatory life-management regime.
- A guide to official resources, not an eligibility adjudicator.
- A planning companion, not a lender, insurer, therapist, doctor, lawyer, or government agency.

## Primary audience
Adults who voluntarily want more clarity in one or more areas of life. The product must work across income levels, household structures, employment types, locations, and life stages. It must not assume that a person is in crisis or classify them by vulnerability.

## Success definition
The MVP succeeds if a user can voluntarily enter a small amount of context, receive a transparent and useful next-step plan, understand the source and uncertainty behind recommendations, and remain fully in control of every action and disclosure.

## Non-promises
SANDAR does not promise income, employment, benefits approval, debt relief, improved health, legal outcomes, or a guaranteed level of resilience.
