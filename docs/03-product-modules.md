# 03 — Product Modules

The six modules are product areas, not six mandatory steps. A user can use one module independently.

## 1. Financial Relief
**Purpose:** Help a user see near-term cash flow and choose a manageable next step.

MVP capabilities:
- User-entered income and expense items, including irregular or uncertain amounts.
- Due-date and priority view controlled by the user.
- Simple cash-flow projection with visible assumptions.
- What-if scenarios (change an amount/date; compare outcomes).
- A list of questions to ask a creditor/provider, without contacting them.

Boundaries: no bank scraping, account aggregation, lending, investment recommendations, credit scoring, or claim that a payment should be skipped. Show arithmetic and assumptions; invite users to verify contractual consequences.

## 2. Public Support & Resource Navigator
**Purpose:** Help users find relevant public services and trusted community resources.

MVP capabilities:
- Search by service topic and user-selected jurisdiction.
- Directory entries with official source, issuing body, coverage area, eligibility text as published, required documents if confirmed, application route, contact route, and last-checked date.
- Save/bookmark a resource privately.
- Flag stale or conflicting information for review.

Boundaries: do not infer eligibility. Distinguish “may be relevant” from “you qualify.” Prefer official Indonesian government sources; link out to the issuing authority.

## 3. Opportunity & Skills Navigator
**Purpose:** Help users discover learning, work, and income opportunities they choose to explore.

MVP capabilities:
- Public opportunity catalogue from official or clearly identified publishers.
- Filters chosen by user: topic, location/remote, deadline, prerequisites, cost, and format.
- Skills-to-opportunity mapping based only on skills the user entered.
- A practical preparation checklist.

Boundaries: no scraping private profiles, no guaranteed job/income claims, no automatic applications, and no discriminatory ranking.

## 4. Family Balance
**Purpose:** Support shared planning only among people who explicitly choose to participate.

MVP capabilities:
- Shared goal or task board.
- Invitation and explicit acceptance.
- Per-person permissions and clear ownership of entries.
- Separate private and shared spaces.

Boundaries: no hidden monitoring, location tracking, coercive controls, or disclosure of one person's private data to another.

## 5. Wellbeing & Capacity
**Purpose:** Let users reflect on their available time, energy, and workload so plans are realistic.

MVP capabilities:
- Optional self-check-in using user-defined scales or simple words.
- Workload and rest planning.
- Ability to reduce, defer, or split tasks.
- Optional links to reputable support resources.

Boundaries: no diagnosis, treatment, crisis prediction, or mental-health profiling. If a user expresses immediate danger, prioritize safe human and emergency support appropriate to their location; do not imply SANDAR is an emergency service.

## 6. Future Resilience
**Purpose:** Help users prepare for uncertainty through goals, buffers, and contingency plans.

MVP capabilities:
- User-defined goals and review dates.
- Scenario planning for changes in income, expenses, time, or responsibilities.
- User-owned contingency checklist.
- Progress review without shame or streak pressure.

Boundaries: no prediction certainty, insurance or investment advice, or ranking users by resilience.

## Cross-module interaction
A user may choose to connect two modules, such as cash-flow planning with a learning opportunity. Explain which user-entered fields are being reused and allow the user to opt out. No cross-module reuse by default.
